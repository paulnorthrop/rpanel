# A brief tribute to Adrian Bowman

Adrian Bowman (1955-2026) created the `rpanel` package approximately 20
years ago, several years before the development of R Shiny. `rpanel`
provides simple GUI controls for R functions, which are useful for
animating graphical displays interactively to illustrate statistical
methods and concepts. Several such “cartoon” functions are included in
`rpanel`, creating visualisations ranging from simple statistical tests
to simulation of Gaussian random fields. `rpanel` enables users to
create their own visualisations and there are 12 R packages on CRAN
Bioconductor that depend on `rpanel` to do this. `rpanel` has proved
useful for teaching statistical ideas, visualising statistical results
and informing choices of tuning parameters in statistical modelling.

Adrian was recognized for his work in nonparametric smoothing,
generalized linear models, statistical graphics and computational
statistics. His book *Applied Smoothing Techniques for Data Analysis*,
and accompanying R package `sm`, written with Adelchi Azzalini, became a
standard text in this area. He spent more than 30 years at the
University of Glasgow, playing a key role in developing the School of
Mathematics and Statistics. Adrian was highly and warmly regarded by
many, as both a statistician and as a person.
